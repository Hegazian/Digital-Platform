# Teacher authoring requirements

**Current direction: 21 October 2026.** M0 engineering is finalized for internal synthetic data; production onboarding remains gated by G1–G3 and operator verification. See [M0 implementation status](docs/M0_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md). Previous content is [superseded and preserved](docs/archive/teacher.md).

The approved workflow is [product scope](docs/PRODUCT_SCOPE.md), [ENH-06 authoring](docs/tasks/ENH-06.md), [ENH-04 imports](docs/tasks/ENH-04.md), [LRN-05 revisions](docs/tasks/LRN-05.md) and [ENH-05 provenance](docs/tasks/ENH-05.md). Teachers manage owned/assigned content and supply all educational text/media/translations/captions/keys/rubrics. Admin review is required for initial publication and subsequent changes.

Advanced collaboration/execution and automatic payment integrations remain staged. No educational generation feature is required.
